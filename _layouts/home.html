Research

My research lies at the intersection of stochastic processes and distributed computing, with a focus on opinion dynamics. I study how simple local rules for updating opinions produce collective behavior in a network, and how quickly these processes reach consensus.

I am particularly interested in minority dynamics and in the roles of sampling and synchrony. My work examines how convergence time depends on the number of opinions each agent samples and on whether updates occur synchronously, semi-synchronously, or asynchronously.
