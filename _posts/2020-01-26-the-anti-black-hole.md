Powerful and important characters from most novels or TV shows usually have some sort of traumatic experience that makes them so dedicated and focused towards their goals. Be it the protagonist or antagonist, these experiences are quite often negative. These events represent a turning point in the mindset of these characters. The way they think, act and behave changes completely. Suddenly, they have some sort of deep motivating factor that just doesn't let them burn out until they achieve their goals or draw their last breath, whichever comes first. Often, these powerful emotions are drawn from revenge. Be it the death of a family member (Batman and his parents) or that of a long-lost love (Raavan and Vedavati), the characters set out on a path to get revenge. Raavan reacts negatively and sets out to destroy the land of Sapt Sindhu (the Indian subcontinent according to Hindu mythology) , while Bruce Wayne becomes the Batman and dedicates his life to protecting the innocent. Both are definitely extreme reactions. These experiences change our characters at the grass root level resulting in my favourite vigilante and my favourite villain.
Every decision these characters have made in their lives ever since their respective tragedies has been towards their one true purpose. Somehow, these events give them purpose in life. More importantly, they never forget these dark moments. They never move on. It becomes an obsession. Despite me taking the example of an antagonist and a protagonist, neither of them lead happy lives by any normal standards. My purpose of writing this is to eventually decipher why they don't move on. I know I have never experienced such dark moments in my life and I hopefully don't in the future as well. So I can't really relate. But shouldn't they be able to move on? How do they stay so obsessed over one tragic event for years? Nay, decades.

I can't stay fixated on a single feeling for more than a day and even that is a stretch. At the end of the day I'm almost forcing myself to keep feeling it. I'm wondering about this because every powerful character from every novel, TV show/anime or movie has been driven by some deep thought. Its almost like some sort of anchor point to which they go to for strength. When faced with adversity or a tough decision, they base their actions on what they've learnt from their dark past. First of all, is it even necessary to have a dark past to be able to derive strength? I believe not, because strength can be drawn from other things as well. Things like the desire to protect something or someone. In fact, I believe this source is perhaps the deepest of all sources of strength (like how parents protect their children).

I think everyone needs a place from where they can derive strength. It is important to establish this source. This infinite source. This source is sort of like the opposite of a black hole. A source reserved for each individual, to where he/she goes when they run out of things to bank on. I've just realized that defining this source is very important for achieving one's life goals. It need not be based on a negative experience, because not everyone has the opportunity to experience one. Another thing is, even though I say that one has to define this anti-black hole for themselves, I also believe that it is probably an extremely challenging task to do by force. I believe it is something that must happen naturally. The anti-black hole cannot be created or invented by you and me, but can only be discovered in due time. Perhaps all we can really do is just keep an eye out and hope that one day we finally cross paths with our oasis in the desert.
